Arbitration Laws

Arbitration provides an alternative mechanism for resolving commercial and contractual disputes. Legal assistance may be provided in disputes arising from agreements, business transactions, and contractual obligations. Representation can include preparation of claims, responses, and supporting legal submissions. The terms of the agreement and applicable arbitration provisions are carefully examined. Assistance may also be provided during hearings and other stages of arbitral proceedings. The objective is to pursue an efficient and legally sound resolution of the dispute.
