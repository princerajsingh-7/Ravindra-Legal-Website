Criminal Laws

Criminal Law deals with offences affecting individuals, society, and public order. Legal assistance may be provided in matters involving theft, assault, cheating, fraud, and other criminal offences. Representation can be undertaken during investigation, bail proceedings, and criminal trials.
Assistance is also available in anticipatory bail and regular bail matters. Cases involving serious offences require careful examination of evidence and applicable provisions. The focus remains on protecting legal rights and ensuring fair representation before the court.
